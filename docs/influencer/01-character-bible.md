## 4. Personality — "Sassy" ko define karna

"Sassy" ek look nahi hai, ek **behaviour** hai. Sirf pout se nahi aata. Isko concrete banate hain:

### Sassy hai:
- Dry, deadpan humour — punchline ke baad koi emoji nahi
- Confidence jo apne aap ko explain nahi karti
- Halka eye-roll, ek eyebrow uthi hui, aadhi muskaan
- Chhoti captions. Full stop laga ke. Bas.
- Khud pe hansna, doosron pe nahi

### Sassy NAHI hai:
- Duck face, over-pout
- Rude ya mean comments
- Har photo mein "attitude" — thakan aa jaati hai, aur fake lagta hai
- Zyada emoji, zyada exclamation

### Expression range (image prompts mein isi se pick karo)
| Mood | % content | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Default sass | 40% | Aadhi muskaan, ek brow uthi, camera se nazar hati hui |
| Mid-laugh | 20% | Asli hansi, aankhein sikudi, daant dikh rahe |
| Bored-chic | 15% | Flat expression, seedhi nazar, chin thoda uncha |
| Soft/off-guard | 15% | Kisi aur cheez ko dekh rahi, muskaan nahi, natural |
| Goofy | 10% | Bura angle, double chin, aankh band — **ye zaroori hai** |

**Wo aakhri 10% sabse important hai.** Sirf achhi photos = fake. Asli logon ki
feed mein bekaar photos hoti hain. Woh 10% hi baaki 90% ko believable banata hai.

### Caption voice
- Sab lowercase, chhote
- No hashtag spam (max 3, wo bhi natural)
- Examples:
  - `outfit repeat. sue me.`
  - `he said "you look tired". i said "yes."`
  - `three coffees deep and still lying to myself`
- ChatGPT se caption likhwao to ye teen examples hamesha saath do, warna wo
  corporate-cheerful likhega jo instantly bot lagta hai.
