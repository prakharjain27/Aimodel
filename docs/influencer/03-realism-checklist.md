# Realism QC Checklist — har image pe chalao

> Tumne kaha tha "chhota se chhota point dhyaan mein rakhna hai". Ye wahi hai.
> **Regel:** ek bhi ❌ = image reject. Fix karne ki koshish mat karo, dobara generate karo.
> 5 badhiya images, 50 theek-thaak images se kahin behtar hain.

**Kaise dekho:** image ko 400% zoom karo aur phone ko ulta karke dekho (upside down).
Ulta dekhne se dimaag "chehra" pehchanna band kar deta hai aur sirf shapes dikhte hain —
galtiyan turant nazar aati hain.

---

## TIER 1 — Instant giveaways (ye 90% AI images ko pakadte hain)

### ☐ 1. Haath aur ungliyan
- [ ] Har haath mein theek **5** ungliyan
- [ ] Ungliyon ki lambai ka order sahi (middle sabse lambi, phir index/ring, phir little)
- [ ] **Knuckles pe creases** hain? AI aksar plastic-smooth knuckles banata hai
- [ ] Nakhoon ka bed dikhta hai, cuticle line hai
- [ ] Angoothe ka joint sahi disha mein muda hai
- [ ] Kisi cheez ko pakda hai to ungliyan uske **around** hain, andar ghusi nahi
- [ ] Dono haath same size ke hain
> **Sabse safe fix:** haath pocket mein, peeth peeche, ya frame ke bahar. Par hamesha
> chhupaoge to bhi shak hoga — kabhi-kabhi saaf haath dikhane hi padenge.

### ☐ 2. Daant
- [ ] Har daant **thoda alag** hai — size, angle, shade
- [ ] Paper-white nahi, natural warm off-white
- [ ] Daanton ki ginti normal (upar 8-10 dikhte hain muskaan mein)
- [ ] Gums ki line uneven hai, perfect arc nahi
- [ ] **Ira ka locked detail:** upar wala left lateral incisor halka ghooma hua
> Perfect Hollywood daant sabse bada single giveaway hai muskaan wali photos mein.

### ☐ 3. Aankhein
- [ ] **Dono aankhon mein catchlight (roshni ka bindu) same disha aur same shape ka hai**
  — ye AI ka sabse common miss hai. Ek aankh mein window, doosri mein ring light = dead
- [ ] Catchlight ki position scene ki light source se match karti hai
- [ ] Dono iris ka rang bilkul same hai
- [ ] Pupils dono same size (jab tak flash na ho)
- [ ] Lower waterline halka pink/geela hai, sookha nahi
- [ ] Palkein uneven hain, comb kiye hue nahi
- [ ] Aankh ka safed hissa **pure white nahi** — halke blood vessels, halka warm tinge

### ☐ 4. Kaan
- [ ] Dono kaan same shape aur size
- [ ] **Ira: left mein 2 piercing, right mein 1** ✱ ye har baar check karo
- [ ] Earrings dono taraf same design (jab tak jaan-boojh ke mismatch na ho)
- [ ] Earring ka post lobe se **actually guzar raha hai**, chipka hua nahi
- [ ] Kaan ki inner ridges (helix, tragus) proper hain, mush nahi

### ☐ 5. Baal
- [ ] Flyaways aur frizz hain (ek bhi nahi = AI)
- [ ] Roots ends se thode gehre hain
- [ ] Hairline pe **baby hairs** hain, sharp cut nahi
- [ ] Baal scalp se nikal rahe hain — helmet ki tarah chipke nahi
- [ ] Curls/waves **irregular** hain, har lat alag
- [ ] Kandhe pe girne wale baal kapde ke saath sahi interact kar rahe hain, andar nahi ghus rahe
- [ ] Part line sidhi-perfect nahi hai

## TIER 2 — Physics aur light

### ☐ 6. Parchhaiyan (shadows)
- [ ] Chehre pe shadow **ek hi** light direction se aa rahi hai
- [ ] Naak ki shadow, chin ki shadow, aur background wall ki shadow — teeno same taraf
- [ ] **Contact shadow** hai jahan pair zameen se milte hain (AI aksar bhool jaata hai —
      insaan tairta hua lagta hai)
- [ ] Baithi hai to kushan mein dabav ka nishaan hai
- [ ] Shadow ki edge softness light source se match karti hai (dhoop = sharp, badal = soft)

### ☐ 7. Pratibimb (reflections)
- [ ] Mirror selfie: **phone reflection mein dikh raha hai**
- [ ] Reflection mein wahi kapde, wahi hairstyle, wahi haath position
- [ ] Reflection LEFT-RIGHT flipped hai (text ulta hoga)
- [ ] Chashma/sunglasses mein reflection scene se match karta hai
- [ ] Paani/glass surface mein reflection sahi angle pe hai

### ☐ 8. Perspective aur lens
- [ ] **Selfie hai to wide-angle distortion hona chahiye** — naak thodi badi, kaan chhote,
      chehra thoda stretched. Perfectly proportioned "selfie" fake hai
- [ ] Door se li gayi photo mein compression hai — chehra thoda flat
- [ ] Depth of field consistent — agar chehra sharp hai to usi plane ka kaan bhi sharp ho
- [ ] Background ki lines (deewar, darwaza, tiles) straight aur convergent hain

### ☐ 9. Kapde aur fabric
- [ ] **Seams aur stitching** dikhte hain aur continuous hain
- [ ] Buttons ke saamne buttonholes hain
- [ ] Zipper ke daant uniform hain, aur zipper pull hai
- [ ] Fabric gravity se sahi lat-ka hua hai
- [ ] Body ke movement se natural wrinkles hain (bilkul flat kapda = 3D render)
- [ ] Kapde ka pattern seam pe **align** hota hai ya jaan-boojh ke nahi — bekaar se distort nahi
- [ ] Straps kandhe pe **rakhi** hain, skin mein merge nahi ho rahi
- [ ] Kapde par likha text **padha ja sakta hai** — ya bilkul nahi hai

### ☐ 10. Zewar
- [ ] Chain ki har kadi (link) consistent hai
- [ ] Chain gale ke around **jaati** hai, chipki nahi
- [ ] Chain gravity follow karti hai — sabse neechla point centre pe
- [ ] Ring ungli ke around hai, floating nahi
- [ ] **GHADI MAT PEHNAO** — AI kabhi watch face sahi nahi banata (numbers, hands, brand
      sab garbage). Ye single detail har baar pakdi jaati hai

## TIER 3 — Background aur scene

### ☐ 11. Text — sabse bada background killer
- [ ] Kahin bhi **koi padha na jaane wala text nahi** hai — signboard, menu, packaging,
      book, poster, number plate, brand logo
- [ ] Jo text hai wo bilkul sahi spelled hai
> **Rule:** background mein text ko **saaf mana karo** prompt mein, ya itna blur karo ki
> shape hi na bane. Aadha-padha text = 100% detection.

### ☐ 12. Background objects
- [ ] Peeche koi aadha insaan / merged limbs / teen tange nahi
- [ ] Peeche log distinct hain, aapas mein ghule nahi
- [ ] Seedhi lines seedhi hain (tiles, door frame, railing)
- [ ] Repeating patterns (tiles, bricks) glitch nahi kar rahe
- [ ] Objects ka scale sahi hai — kursi bandi ke hisaab se sahi size
- [ ] Plant ke patte alag-alag hain, ek blob nahi

### ☐ 13. Body proportions
- [ ] Sar : body = roughly 1 : 7.5
- [ ] Haath **jaangh ke beech** tak aate hain jab neeche latke hon
- [ ] Kandhe ki chaudai ~2 sar ke barabar
- [ ] Kohni kamar ke level pe
- [ ] Pair ka size ≈ forearm ki lambai (chhote pair AI ki aadat hai)
- [ ] Gardan ki lambai natural hai — AI aksar lambi karta hai
- [ ] Dono taange same lambai, ghutne same height pe

### ☐ 14. Pair (agar dikhein)
- [ ] 5 ungliyan, ghatta hua size order
- [ ] Toenails hain
- [ ] Sandal ki strap ungliyon ke **beech** se guzar rahi hai
- [ ] Talwe zameen ko chhoo rahe hain (contact shadow!)

## TIER 4 — Image forensics (jo tools pakadte hain)

### ☐ 15. Skin — sabse gehra tell
- [ ] **Pores dikhte hain** — cheeks aur naak pe zyada, forehead pe kam
- [ ] Skin tone **uneven** hai — chehre ke alag hisson mein alag
- [ ] **Vellus hair (peach fuzz)** hai jawline aur upper lip pe, backlight mein
- [ ] Koi na koi imperfection hai: ek daana, halka scar, dry patch, redness
- [ ] Under-eye area mein texture hai
- [ ] Nasolabial fold (naak se hoth tak) hai — chahe halka
- [ ] Gardan aur chehre ka tone **match** karta hai (AI aksar gardan halki chhod deta hai)
- [ ] Chehra aur haath ki skin same person ki lagti hai

### ☐ 16. Image ki technical quality
- [ ] **Sharpness uniform NAHI hai** — ek jagah focus, baaki halka soft
- [ ] Shadows mein grain/noise hai
- [ ] Halka chromatic aberration high-contrast edges pe
- [ ] Image **over-sharpened nahi** hai (AI ka signature halo edges pe)
- [ ] Highlights kahin clip ho rahe hain (asli camera karta hai)
- [ ] Resolution absurdly high nahi hai — phone photo phone photo lage

### ☐ 17. Metadata — post karne se pehle
- [ ] **EXIF strip kiya** (AI generators C2PA/provenance tags embed karte hain)
- [ ] File ka naam generic hai (`IMG_4471.jpg`, na ki `generated_image_final_v3.png`)
- [ ] File size phone photo jaisa hai (2-5 MB, 200 KB nahi)
- [ ] Format JPEG hai, PNG nahi — **phone kabhi PNG nahi deta**, ye bada tell hai
> Command: `exiftool -all= image.jpg` — ya online EXIF remover.
> Chaho to main is repo mein ek chhota script likh doon jo ye poora post-process karta ho.

## TIER 5 — Account-level realism (individual image se bhi bada)

Log ek image se nahi, **pattern** se pakadte hain.

- [ ] Har photo perfect nahi hai — **10% jaan-boojh ke bekaar** photos
- [ ] Outfits repeat hote hain (asli log ek hi jeans 50 baar pehente hain)
- [ ] Same ghar, same deewar, same bedsheet baar-baar aata hai
- [ ] Har din nayi jagah nahi — asli zindagi boring hoti hai
- [ ] Posting times IST waking hours mein, aur **irregular** hain
- [ ] Weather post ki date se match karta hai
- [ ] Photos mein aage-peeche continuity hai (chipped nail paint agle din bhi chipped)
- [ ] Comments ka jawab deti hai, aur jawab **short aur specific** hote hain
- [ ] Kabhi-kabhi text-only story, blurry pet photo, khana — sirf chehra nahi
- [ ] Same "friend" baar-baar aata hai (haath/kandha/peeche se)
- [ ] Growth organic hai — 0 se 50k ek hafte mein = instant report

---

## Quick 20-second check (jaldi mein ho to bas ye)

1. **Haath** — 5 ungliyan?
2. **Aankhon ka catchlight** — dono same?
3. **Kaan** — left 2 piercing, right 1?
4. **Background text** — koi padha na jaane wala text?
5. **Skin** — pores dikh rahe hain?
6. **Contact shadow** — pair/kursi ke neeche shadow hai?
7. **Mole M1** — left cheekbone pe hai, sahi jagah?

Saat mein se saat pass = post karne layak. Ek bhi fail = dobara generate.
