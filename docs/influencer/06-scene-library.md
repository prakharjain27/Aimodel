# Scene Library — sab ready, bas paste karo

**Use kaise karein:** har scene ke aage ye lagao —
```
Same character, same locked face. New scene:
[SCENE]
```

**Har scene ke ant mein ye line add karna (ye sabse zyada kaam karti hai):**
```
Phone camera, unedited, mild grain, no retouching. It should look like a photo that
would not stand out on anyone's camera roll.
```

## Strategy note — chaaron niche ek saath?

Tumne "All" chuna. Seedhi baat: **Instagram ka algorithm ek clear signal maangta hai.**
Chaar niche mix karoge to reach dheemi hogi kyunki algorithm samajh nahi paayega
kisko dikhaye.

**Meri raay:** shuru **Fashion (A)** se karo — 70% content wahi. Baaki teen ko
10-10% "flavour" ki tarah use karo (Ira ki zindagi ka hissa, alag content pillar nahi).
Jo category pe engagement zyada aaye, uspe shift kar jaana.

Neeche chaaron ka poora content hai — tumhara call.

## A · FASHION + LIFESTYLE (primary — 70%)

**A1 — Outfit mirror check**
```
She is standing in her bedroom in front of a full-length mirror leaning against a beige
wall, taking a mirror photo on her phone. She wears wide-leg black trousers and a fitted
white tank. Her phone covers part of her chin. Behind her the bed is unmade and a beige
towel is on the chair. Warm overhead bulb light with downward shadows.
```

**A2 — Getting-ready chaos**
```
She is sitting cross-legged on her bedroom floor surrounded by three rejected outfits
thrown on the bed behind her. She is mid-sentence, one hand gesturing, wearing only a
plain grey t-shirt and shorts, hair in a messy claw clip with pieces falling out. Flat
afternoon light from a window on the right.
```

**A3 — Street style, walking**
```
She is walking on a Mumbai footpath, caught mid-stride by someone half a step behind her.
She wears an oversized cream linen shirt over a black tank and straight jeans, canvas
tote on her shoulder. There is slight motion blur on her hand and hair. Overcast light.
The background is a plain wall and a parked scooter, no readable signage.
```

**A4 — Outfit detail, no face**
```
A close crop from her chest to her knees only — no face in frame. She wears a dark green
midi dress with a small stain near the hem she hasn't noticed. Her hands are adjusting the
waistband; five fingers on each hand, short bare nails, thin gold ring on her right index
finger. Flat window light.
```

**A5 — Thrift haul**
```
She is sitting on the floor of her flat with four thrifted garments spread around her,
holding one up to the camera with a flat, unimpressed expression. Beige wall, terracotta
plant pot visible at the edge of the frame. Late afternoon light, slightly warm.
```

**A6 — Rain, ruined outfit**
```
She is standing under an awning during Mumbai monsoon rain, her linen shirt soaked and
sticking, hair wet and flat against her head, mascara slightly smudged under one eye.
She is looking at the rain, not the camera, with an expression of pure resignation. Grey
overcast light, wet reflective pavement.
```

**A7 — Bad photo, posted anyway**
```
A badly framed photo of her — too much space above her head, she is slightly out of
focus, caught mid-blink with her mouth half open. She is wearing a going-out outfit and
was clearly about to leave. Harsh direct flash in a dim hallway.
```

## B · FITNESS (10%)

> ⚠️ Body shots mein AI errors sabse zyada aate hain. Har fitness image pe
> `03-realism-checklist.md` **do baar** chalao — khaaskar haath, proportions, aur gym
> equipment pe likha text.

**B1 — Post-workout, unglamorous**
```
She has just finished a workout and is sitting on a gym bench, elbows on knees, head
down, breathing hard. Her face is flushed red and shiny with sweat, hair stuck to her
temples and neck, a few flyaways escaping her ponytail. She wears a plain black sports
bra and grey shorts. Harsh overhead fluorescent gym lighting from above, creating
shadows under her eyes and nose. No equipment branding or text visible anywhere.
```

**B2 — Home stretch**
```
She is on a yoga mat on her living room floor, mid-stretch, one leg extended. She is
looking at her phone propped against the wall, not at the camera. Plain beige wall,
morning light from a window on the left. She wears mismatched workout clothes — a faded
college t-shirt and black leggings.
```

**B3 — Morning run, no makeup**
```
A phone selfie taken outdoors after a run, arm's length, wide-angle distortion making her
nose slightly larger. Her face is bare, flushed, slightly sweaty, hair pulled back with
loose strands everywhere. She is squinting slightly in the flat morning light. The
background is a blurred park with no signage.
```

**B4 — Rest day honesty**
```
She is lying on her sofa in a hoodie and shorts, one arm behind her head, looking at the
camera with a flat expression. There is a plate with crumbs on the coffee table. Warm
lamp light, evening. Her hair is unwashed and pushed back.
```

## C · TRAVEL (10%)

> ⚠️ **Timezone aur weather continuity yahan sabse bada risk hai.** Agar Goa post kar
> rahe ho to agle 3-4 din ka content bhi Goa ka hona chahiye. Ek din Mumbai, agle din
> Manali, phir wapas Mumbai = 100% bot signal.

**C1 — Train window**
```
She is sitting by a train window, chin resting on her hand, looking out at blurred green
countryside. Her hair is messy from the wind through the window. Overcast daylight coming
from the window on her left, leaving the right side of her face in shadow. She wears a
crumpled oversized shirt. No text visible on the train interior.
```

**C2 — Airport, tired**
```
A phone selfie in an airport waiting area at 5am. Harsh overhead fluorescent light,
unflattering, showing every bit of under-eye shadow. Her expression is completely flat.
Hair in a low messy bun, oversized hoodie. The background is intentionally blurred with
no readable signage or boarding information.
```

**C3 — Beach, wind, no pose**
```
She is on a beach at Goa in the late afternoon, hair completely thrown across her face by
the wind, one hand trying to pull it away, laughing at how badly it's going. She wears a
loose white shirt over a swimsuit. Warm low sunlight from behind her creating rim light on
her hair and visible peach fuzz along her jaw. Sand on her feet, five toes each, contact
shadows where she stands.
```

**C4 — Hotel room, mundane**
```
She is sitting on the edge of a plain hotel bed, still in yesterday's clothes, phone in
one hand, looking exhausted. The room is unremarkable — beige walls, a lamp, an open
suitcase with clothes spilling out. Warm bedside lamp light only, quite dim, with
noticeable grain in the shadows.
```

## D · BEAUTY / SKINCARE (10%)

> ⚠️ Sabse mushkil category. Close-up mein skin texture pe maximum scrutiny hoti hai.
> **Har beauty image pe skin section (Tier 4, point 15) line-by-line check karo.**
> Ek bhi smooth patch = reject.

**D1 — Bare face, honest**
```
A close-up phone photo of her face with absolutely no makeup, taken in flat grey daylight
from a window. Her skin shows everything: open pores across her nose and cheeks, slight
redness around her nostrils and chin, one small healing spot on her jaw, faint under-eye
darkness, and visible fine peach fuzz along her jawline and upper lip. Her eyebrows are
unshaped. Her expression is neutral, slightly unimpressed. This is not a flattering photo
and it is not meant to be.
```

**D2 — Mid-routine**
```
A close-up of her face with a white cream partially rubbed in on one cheek and still
visible as a streak on the other. Her hair is pushed back with a fabric headband. She is
looking slightly off-camera. Warm bathroom bulb light from above. Her skin is damp.
```

**D3 — Makeup half done**
```
A close-up showing one eye with full eye makeup done and the other completely bare — the
contrast is the point. Her mouth is slightly open in concentration. Bathroom mirror light
from the front, flat and even. Visible skin texture on both sides.
```

**D4 — End of day**
```
A close-up of her face late at night under a warm lamp, makeup half removed, mascara
smudged under both eyes, lipstick worn away except at the outer edges of her lips. She
looks tired. Grain is heavy because the light is low.
```

## E · FILLER CONTENT (feed ko real banata hai)

Ye **utna hi zaroori** hai jitna face content. Asli logon ki feed mein sirf chehra
nahi hota. Ye posts algorithm ke liye bhi accha hai aur "bot" shak bhi todte hain.

**E1** — `A phone photo of a cup of cutting chai on a wet table, someone's hand in frame holding it. Overcast light.`

**E2** — `A badly framed photo of a street cat sitting on a parked scooter seat, slightly out of focus, taken quickly.`

**E3** — `A messy desk from above — laptop, a notebook with unreadable handwriting, three pens, an empty coffee cup, phone charger cable tangled. Warm lamp light.`

**E4** — `A window view of Mumbai rain at night, streetlights blurred through wet glass, slightly out of focus, heavy grain.`

**E5** — `A close-up of her hands holding a paperback book, five fingers visible on each, short bare nails, thin gold ring on the right index finger. Soft window light. The book cover is turned away so no text is visible.`

**E6** — `Two pairs of feet on a footpath from above — hers in scuffed white sneakers and a friend's in sandals. Mid-step, slight motion blur.`

### Posting rules
- Din mein **1** post, hafte mein 1 din **chhod do** (asli log roz post nahi karte)
- Time **IST 9am–11pm** ke andar, aur **irregular** — roz 7pm sharp = bot
- Outfits **repeat** karo — A1 wali jeans A3 aur A5 mein bhi aani chahiye
- Ek hi ghar, ek hi deewar, ek hi bedsheet baar-baar
- Weather post ki date se match kare (Mumbai Jun–Sep = baarish)
- Comments ka jawab **short aur specific** do — generic "thank you 😍" bot ka signal hai

### Captions
Har caption `01-character-bible.md` ki voice mein — lowercase, chhoti, dry.
ChatGPT se likhwao to ye teen examples hamesha saath do, warna wo corporate-cheerful
likhega jo turant fake lagta hai:
```
outfit repeat. sue me.
he said "you look tired". i said "yes."
three coffees deep and still lying to myself
```
